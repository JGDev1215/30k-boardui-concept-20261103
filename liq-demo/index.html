About this demo

Practice the decision flow with made-up prices.

Five invented 23-hour sessions, Sunday 4 Oct 22:00 UTC through Friday 9 Oct 21:00 UTC: 6,900 consecutive one-minute bars within sessions, with a one-hour daily pause. Each day has one playable checkpoint at 12:00 UTC, using the session high and low known then.

All prices and levels are invented. This public static page lets anyone inspect future data before a choice. It does not place trades or save decisions.
